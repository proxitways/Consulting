FAQ

Before you begin.

Can Elepta guarantee admission, an Ausbildung contract or a visa?

No. Decisions are made by universities, employers and the relevant German authorities. Our role is to help you plan, prepare and organise a stronger, complete application.

Which Germany pathway is right for me?

That depends on factors such as your qualifications, professional experience, language level, finances and goal. Start with a profile assessment so the options can be compared properly.

Do requirements stay the same?

No. University, employer and immigration requirements can change. Your case should be checked against current official sources before submission.

Do you provide legal representation?

Elepta Consulting provides education, application and administrative support. Where regulated legal advice or representation is required, you should use an appropriately qualified professional.
