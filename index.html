German Language

From Hallo to confident German. 🇩🇪

Start at the level that suits you and build progressively through A1, A2, B1 and B2.

A1

Beginner

Introductions, everyday vocabulary, basic grammar and simple conversations.

A2

Elementary

Handle common everyday situations and build stronger sentence structures.

B1

Intermediate

Communicate more independently about familiar topics, work and daily life.

B2

Upper-intermediate

Develop more fluent, detailed communication and understand more complex German.
